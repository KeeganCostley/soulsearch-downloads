SoulSearch

SoulSearch syncs your liked and saved songs on Spotify, SoundCloud, YouTube and Apple Music (including Shazam). Each new like or updated playlist triggers an automated search on Soulseek, targeting your preferred audio file type and quality. It then puts the file into your Rekordbox folder. You just need to refresh the playlist and your track is ready to play.
