SoulSearch

Watches what you like on Spotify, SoundCloud, YouTube, Apple Music and Shazam. Finds the files on Soulseek. Puts them in Rekordbox.

Sync_□x
What it does_□x
  • Likes and playlists from Spotify, SoundCloud and YouTube. Shared Apple Music playlists. Shazam, through a file you export.
  • Tries Soulseek, then SoundCloud, then another version of the track (extended, edit, remix).
  • Every source gets its own playlist in Rekordbox.
  • Crate digging: type a DJ, or one set, and it finds the tracklists and fetches them. Uses your Claude account, or you can paste its brief into any other AI.
  • If your internet drops, it waits and carries on. Nothing gets written off as missing.
First run_□x

Your computer won't recognise the app. It isn't signed yet.

Windows
  1. Open SoulSearch.exe.
  2. On the blue warning, click More info, then Run anyway.
Mac
  1. Unzip it and drag SoulSearch to Applications.
  2. Right-click it, Open, then Open again.
  3. Still blocked: System Settings, Privacy & Security, Open Anyway.

You need Rekordbox. Downloads use real data, so watch it on a hotspot.
