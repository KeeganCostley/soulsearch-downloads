SoulSearch

SoulSearch watches the songs you like and the playlists you add to on Spotify, SoundCloud and more. It searches for each one in the file type and audio quality you choose, downloads it, and puts it in a Rekordbox folder.

WindowsWindows 10 or 11 MacApple Silicon (M1 and newer)
Free. No Intel Mac build yet.Next: setup guide
What it does_□x
  • Likes and playlists from Spotify, SoundCloud, YouTube and Apple Music. Shazam, through a file you export.
  • You pick the file types: MP3, FLAC first with a 320 MP3 backup, or FLAC only.
  • Searches Soulseek. SoundCloud as a backup is optional, and so is another version of the track (extended, edit, remix).
  • Every source gets its own playlist in Rekordbox.
  • Crate digging: type a DJ, or one set, and it finds the tracklists and fetches them. Uses your Claude account, or you can paste its brief into any other AI.
  • If your internet drops, it waits and carries on. Nothing gets written off as missing.
