SoulSearch syncs your liked and saved songs on Spotify, SoundCloud, YouTube and Apple Music (including Shazam). Each new like or updated playlist triggers an automated search on Soulseek, downloading your preferred audio file type and quality. It then puts the files into your Rekordbox folder. You just need to refresh the playlist and your tracks are ready to play.

SoulSearch also has a crate digging feature. Enter the name of a DJ or artist and it pulls every track it can find from their live sets and recorded mixes, then downloads them through the same process into a folder of their own in Rekordbox.
