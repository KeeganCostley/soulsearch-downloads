SoulSearch

SoulSearch watches the songs you like and the playlists you add to on Spotify, SoundCloud and more. It searches for each one in the file type and audio quality you choose, downloads it, and puts it in a Rekordbox folder.

WindowsWindows 10 or 11 MacApple Silicon (M1 and newer)

Free. Apple Silicon Macs only for now

What it does_□x
  • Spotify, SoundCloud, YouTube, Apple Music and Shazam.
  • MP3, FLAC first with a 320 backup, or FLAC only.
  • Every source gets its own playlist in Rekordbox.
  • Crate digging: type a DJ and it finds their sets.
